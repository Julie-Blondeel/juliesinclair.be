About Julie

Julie Sinclair paints small, quiet things — lakes before the wind, birches in June, flowers that never got given away. She works slowly, in the mornings, with the door closed, and finishes very few pieces.

She is shy about everything else. If you would like to know her, look at the paintings — that is where she keeps the things she does not say out loud.
